About event

Northside Frequency is a free hip-hop showcase bringing together producers, MCs, DJs, and fans for a night of raw talent and community energy.

This year's lineup features Immanuel Adi headlining alongside an extraordinary roster of underground artists. Expect unreleased tracks, surprise appearances, and a crowd that lives for the culture.

Join us at International Praise Church as we bring together voices to share ideas, celebrate music, and prove that the best shows don't have a price tag.
